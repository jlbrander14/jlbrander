About

Five years turning retail floors into platforms.

I'm a Technical Product Manager at Kith, where I own the roadmap for internal platforms and ecommerce systems in close partnership with engineering — Shopify catalog integration across storefronts and retail, the proprietary software our warehouse and stores run on, and the shipping platform tying it all to our 3PLs. Before that I was the person people came to for SQL, schema design, and the Snowflake models behind executive reporting.

I didn't start in a product seat. I started as a stock associate — which means I've done the operational work these systems are built to support, not just spec'd it from a distance. That's the same lens I bring to outside projects: understand how the business actually runs before touching the storefront.
